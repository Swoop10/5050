Is it legal for 5050 Real Estate to offer a Buyer commission credit?

Yes, offering a commission credit to a Buyer in a real estate transaction is 100% legal in the State of Arizona, provided it is fully disclosed to all parties of the transaction as well as the lender, and the credit is paid through the licensed brokerage.

The commission credit essentially lowers the Buyer's transactional expenses, such as closing costs, prepaid expenses (like HOA or taxes), etc.

5050 Real Estate cannot and will not offer you cash in hand as the commission credit, because doing so would be illegal. However, the commission credit you receive on your closing costs, prepaid expenses, etc. is a savings and is money you don't have to spend during your transaction.

Why would 5050 Real Estate credit half of your net commission to a Buyer?

The business model is a win-win; 5050 Real Estate gets the opportunity to meet and represent Buyers purchasing a beautiful, brand-new home; Buyers get the opportunity to receive outstanding service and save some money on their beautiful, brand-new home. Everyone wins — we make money representing awesome clients, and you save money purchasing an awesome home.

How long does the whole process typically take, from first contact to Close of Escrow?

It depends. Building a home from scratch could take approximately 6–9 months. However, many Builders have “quick move-in homes” available where the process can be condensed to just a few weeks.

What if I've already toured a model home or registered myself — is it too late to work with you?

Call us — we can discuss your exact situation. Registrations do expire, so there's potentially still a chance to work together. Just call, and we can discuss.

Do I need to be pre-approved for a mortgage before I reach out or tour a model home?

Technically, no — you do not need to be pre-approved beforehand. But it is always a good idea to know how much home you can afford before starting the home purchase process. Pre-approval beforehand is not a bad idea.
